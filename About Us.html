ABOUT US

Mamba Point Resturant was established in 1980 to provide customers with delicious food and excellent service with a view. It is taliored to the people of Sierra Leone with it's spacious dining and relaxing smooth jazz music, and it's expansive menu items. We Aim to continue to provide high-quality food and a wonderful relaxing experience to our customers.

Our Story

Our resturant was created with one simple goal in mind, to provide customers with delicious food and excellent service in a comfortable environment.

Our chefs carefully prepare every meal, starters and dessert using fresh ingredients and recipes inspired by local and international cuisine.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality food, friendly service and memorable dining experiences for every customer.
