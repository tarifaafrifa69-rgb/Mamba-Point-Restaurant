WELCOME TO MAMBA POINT

ABOUT US

Mamba Point Restaurant was established in 1980 to provide customers with delicious food and excellent service with a view. It is tailored to the people of Sierra Leone with its spacious dining, relaxing smooth jazz music, and expansive menu items.

We aim to continue providing high-quality food and a wonderful, relaxing experience to our customers.
